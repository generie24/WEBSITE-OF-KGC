# 2026-10-13

## Booking modal refactor — 4-step wizard (index.html)

Refactored the "Book KGC Services" modal into a 4-step wizard while keeping the dark-gold theme.

**Files changed:** `index.html` only (markup, `<style>`, and portal `<script>`).

- **Stepper:** `.kgc-stepper` tab bar (`#bookingStepper`) — 4 tabs with gold top border (`#FACC15`) for the active step and gold check indicators for completed steps.
- **Step 1 (Service Selection):** two-column layout — `.kgc-cat-list` category sidebar (IT / Marketing / Real Estate / Legal with active pill + counts) and `.kgc-subs-grid` subsidiary cards. Active cards expand to service checkboxes with gold pricing badges + optional per-company "project needs" textarea; inactive cards show a compact "View Services" CTA.
- **Step 2 (Scheduling):** interactive month calendar (`#bookingCalDays`, prev/next month nav, past dates disabled) + time-slot pills + Online Video Call / In-Person format toggles.
- **Step 3 (Your Details):** First/Last name and Email/Phone in 2-col rows, optional Company, full-width Project Details textarea.
- **Step 4 (Review & Confirm):** 4 summary cards (Service Summary, Appointment Details, Estimated Total range, Your Details).
- **Sticky footer:** live summary per step + Previous / Skip for now / primary gold CTA whose label changes per step.

**Architecture notes (important for future edits):**
- Catalog data lives in the JS constant `BOOKING_CATALOG` (categories + subsidiaries + services with `price`/`unit`/`duration`). The modal body is rendered dynamically by `renderBookingCategories`, `renderBookingSubsidiaries`, `renderBookingCalendar`, `renderBookingTimeSlots`, `renderBookingReview`, `renderBookingFooter`, `renderBookingStepper`.
- Wizard state is the `bookingState` object; step navigation via `showBookingStep()` and the `window.nextBookingStep/prevBookingStep/skipBookingStep/goBookingStep` API. Event delegation is bound once on `document` (`handleWizardClick` / `handleWizardChange`).
- **Payload contract:** `POST /api/bookings` `date` must be ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` because Supabase `bookings.service_date` is a real date column. Human-readable schedule + meeting format ride inside `notes`. `supabase-store.js#insertBooking` whitelists columns, so extra payload keys (`meetingFormat`, `timeSlot`, `duration`) are ignored safely.
- Preserved for compatibility: global `bookingFullName` (hidden, derived from first/last), `#bookingStep1..4`, `openBookingDirect`, `preselectCompanyInBooking`, `getSelectedServicesByCompany`, `formatServicesSummary`, `validateSelectedServiceGroups`, `handlePendingBookingSelection`, and the hidden `paymentMethod` radio (defaults to "Corporate Invoice / COD" — the visual payment picker was dropped in the new design).
