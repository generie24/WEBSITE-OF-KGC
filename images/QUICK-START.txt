📁 FOLDER STRUCTURE (READY TO USE):

   images/
   ├── logos/          ← Put 10 company logos here
   └── backgrounds/    ← Put 10 background images here


🎯 WHAT TO DO:

   1. Add your logo images to:      images/logos/
   2. Add your background images to: images/backgrounds/
   3. Open index.html
   4. Search for each "Card X:" (X = 1 to 10)
   5. Replace the 2 image URLs per card
   6. Save and refresh browser


📋 EXAMPLE (Card 1):

   FIND THIS:
   <img src="https://via.placeholder.com/80/9B7FD4/FFFFFF?text=B"
   
   CHANGE TO:
   <img src="images/logos/brains.png"

   ---

   FIND THIS:
   background-image: url('https://images.unsplash.com/...')
   
   CHANGE TO:
   background-image: url('images/backgrounds/brains-bg.jpg')


📏 IMAGE SIZES:

   Logos:       200x200px (PNG with transparency)
   Backgrounds: 1920x1080px (JPG)
