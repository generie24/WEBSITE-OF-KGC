═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  📝 INSTRUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Open index.html in your text editor

2. Press Ctrl+F to open Find

3. Search for: "Card 1: Brains Infinite" (or any card number)

4. You'll find TWO image URLs to change:
   - Logo image:       <img src="URL_HERE"
   - Background image: background-image: url('URL_HERE')

5. Replace the URLs with your own image paths:
   
   LOCAL FILES:
     images/company-logo.png
     images/company-background.jpg
   
   ONLINE URLS:
     https://your-website.com/logo.png
     https://your-website.com/background.jpg

6. Save the file and refresh your browser
